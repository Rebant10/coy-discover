# Part A — Feature Suggestions

I signed up on Coy, went through the OTP login, poked around the home screen, tried adding a crush, checked out the invite/referral system, and looked at the extra-slots pricing page. Here are two features I think would genuinely improve the app.

---

## 1. Give people clues about who added them

Right now when someone adds you as a crush, you just see "No one has added you yet" or a notification that *somebody* did — but that's it. There's no way to narrow it down. Honestly, when I first saw it, my reaction was "cool, but now what?" — I had no next step.

I think Coy should drop small, anonymous hints over a few days. Nothing that gives away who it is — that would break the whole point — but just enough to make it fun:

- Day 1: "Someone added you 👀"  
- Day 2: "They're from Delhi"
- Day 3: "You share 2 mutual friends on Coy"
- Maybe after a week: "Their name starts with A"

**Why I think this matters:**

The biggest issue I see with the current flow is that getting a "someone added you" notification is exciting for about 10 seconds, and then it's a dead end. You can't do anything with it. Hints would give people a reason to come back every day — even if they don't have a crush to add, they'd open the app to check if there's a new clue.

It also fits Coy's whole thing. You're not revealing who the person is. You're just making the guessing game more fun. And if someone *really* wants the next hint right away instead of waiting, that's a pretty natural thing to charge for — way more natural than just selling extra slots.

The other thing I noticed is that when you get the notification, you kind of want to invite more friends just to figure out who it might be by process of elimination. So hints would probably drive invites organically, which is good for growth.

---

## 2. Let crushes expire after a few days

This one hit me when I was looking at the 3 free slots. I kept thinking — if I use all 3, they're gone forever unless I pay. That actually made me *not want to use them*, which is the opposite of what you want.

The idea: each crush auto-expires after 7 days if the other person doesn't add you back. Your slot gets freed up, their notification goes away. Simple.

The receiver would see something like: "Someone added you — 5 days left before it expires." The sender sees: "Your crush expires in 5 days."

**Why I think this matters:**

Right now the slots feel too precious. 3 slots is already very few, and once they're locked in, you're stuck. That makes people play it super safe — they'll only use a slot on someone they're really sure about, which means fewer crushes get sent overall. But Coy's whole value comes from people actually sending crushes. If nobody sends them, nothing happens.

With expiry, using a slot feels less risky. If it doesn't work out in a week, you get it back. That means people would actually use their slots instead of hoarding them.

The countdown also adds urgency for the person on the receiving end. Right now there's zero time pressure — if someone added me, I can just ignore it forever. But if I know the notification disappears in 3 days, I'm way more likely to think about it and maybe add people back. That's just loss aversion doing its thing.

And it keeps the app feeling alive. Without expiry, everything kind of just... sits there. Slots stay locked, old notifications linger. With expiry, there's always movement — crushes cycling in and out, new chances opening up.

You could even monetize it cleanly: let people pay to make a crush permanent (no expiry) for the ones they really care about. The base experience stays free and functional, but there's a clear reason to pay.

---

Both of these build on what Coy already does well — the anonymous crush mechanic — without changing the core promise. They just make the loop of "add crush → curiosity → reciprocate → match" more engaging at every step.
